An efficient line follower robot built to compete in the Meshmerize competition at IIT Bombay, showcasing precision and speed. Secured 9th place.

Video:- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bG0LW3L4kpo

Presented at Techfest, IIT Bombay, 2021

### Interesting things

- I realized that fine-tuning the PID controller for smooth and accurate movement was actually harder than building the robot.
- There were a lot of sensors and not that many pins, so utilized a MUX.
- After experimenting with some sensor configurations, I observed that configurations with increased spacing from center worked better that have constant distance between them
