Our team of 8 designed and built two micromice for the competition at IIT Bombay—one with encoded motors and another with stepper motors. Both utilized infrared sensors. Using the flood-fill algorithm for maze solving, we secured 5th place.

Challenges Faced

- Implementing the flood-fill algorithm for efficient maze navigation.
- Calibrating encoded and stepper motors for precise movements.
- Synchronizing team efforts to design, test, and refine both robots.
