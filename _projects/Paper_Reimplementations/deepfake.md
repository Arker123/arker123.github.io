### Description

This project focused on detecting deepfake images using state-of-the-art deep learning models and datasets like StyleGAN, Celeb-A, FFHQ, and ATTGAN. With an accuracy of approximately 70%, it showcased the potential of AI in combating fake content.

### Challenges Faced

- Training models on diverse datasets to improve detection accuracy.
- Handling variations in image styles and resolutions across datasets.
- Fine-tuning deep learning architectures for optimal performance.
