### Description

For Halloween, we attached a witch artwork to the top of our drone to create a flying scare. While it was meant to spook everyone, it ended up only scaring a dog!


### Challenges Faced

- Balancing the drone's stability with the added weight of the artwork.
- Ensuring the witch design looked spooky while being lightweight.
- Managing unexpected reactions—or lack thereof—from onlookers!
