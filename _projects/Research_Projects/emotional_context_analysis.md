I have developed a project titled **Emotional Context Analysis** in collaboration with **Professors Dr. Abhinav Dhall** and **Dr. Sudeepta Mishra**. The project uses **AR spectacles** to suggest conversational responses based on the desired interaction style, such as **funny** or **romantic**.

#### How It Works:
- The system processes **audio** and **video** inputs from the conversation, analyzing **body language**, the **transcript**, and the **tone of voice** to understand the emotional context.
  
- It then uses this multimodal data to provide suggestions for guiding the conversation, ensuring it matches the preferred interaction style.

- The system was trained using the **IEMOCAP dataset**, which is widely used for emotion recognition tasks, to improve its ability to detect emotions and suggest contextually appropriate responses.

### Conclusion:
This project combines **augmented reality** with **emotion recognition**, offering a novel way to enhance communication by personalizing interactions based on emotional context. It adapts the conversation style, making interactions more engaging and contextually relevant.
