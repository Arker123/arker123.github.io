**AI Fitness Application: Fitlovey**  

Our team developed Fitlovey, an AI-powered fitness application built in Dart. The app leverages a lightweight Vision Transformer (ViT) model to analyze and evaluate the correctness of exercises, providing users with real-time feedback for improved form and performance.
