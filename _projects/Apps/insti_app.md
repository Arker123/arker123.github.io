**Institute App for IIT Ropar**

The Institute App for IIT Ropar is designed to enhance campus life by providing a seamless and connected experience for students. Key features include:

- **Feed**: Stay updated with the latest announcements, events, and updates across campus.
- **Explore**: Discover campus facilities, services, and activities with ease.
- **Blog**: A dedicated space for students to share their stories, projects, and insights with the community.
- **Calendar**: Access an integrated calendar for academic schedules, events, and deadlines.
- **Resources**: Quickly access academic materials like lecture notes, course content, and past year question papers (PYQs).
- **Lost and Found**: Report lost items or help others recover their belongings through this dedicated feature.
- **Mess Updates**: View daily menus and receive real-time notifications about any changes in the mess schedule.
- **Chat**: A platform for students to connect and communicate, overcoming the limitations of other group chat platforms.
- **Quick Links**: Direct access to essential campus services, websites, and tools for added convenience.

This app brings everything students need into one place, making life at IIT Ropar more organized and efficient.
