### Institute App for IIT Ropar  

I created an **Institute App** for IIT Ropar to make campus life easier and more connected. The app includes:  

- **Common Chat**: A platform for all students to chat together, solving the problem of WhatsApp's 1024-member limit.  
- **Mess Updates**: Daily menus and notifications for any changes in the mess schedule.  
- **Academic Resources**: Easy access to lecture notes, course materials, and important announcements.  
- **Student Blogs**: A space where students can share their experiences, ideas, and projects.  
- **Campus Maps**: Interactive maps to help students and visitors find their way around.  
- **Shuttle Service Updates**: Live updates on shuttle timings and routes to make travel on campus more convenient.  
- **Lost and Found**: A platform to report lost items and help others find their belongings.
