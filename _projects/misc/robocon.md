### Robocon 2022 Participation  

As part of **Team IIT Ropar**, we participated in **Robocon 2022**, showcasing two custom-built robots, R1 and R2, designed to tackle dynamic challenges collaboratively.  

- **R1 Bot**:  
  - Equipped with mecanum wheels for omnidirectional movement.  
  - Featured a ball-shooting mechanism using a camera-based target detection system.  

- **R2 Bot**:  
  - Integrated a gantry system for lifting and stacking objects.  
  - Designed to collect and pass balls to R1 using servo-controlled clamps.  

Our team successfully cleared **Round 1** with exceptional performance. Unfortunately, due to COVID-19, we couldn’t proceed to Round 2. Despite the setback, the experience was invaluable, pushing us to innovate and work as a cohesive unit.
